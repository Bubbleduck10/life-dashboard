    👤 Account

    Accounts are coming soon — log in once, and your data follows you on any device automatically. For now, use ☁ Cloud sync or a backup file below.

    Log in and your data saves to your account automatically — open the app anywhere and pick up where you left off.

    Signed in as — your data backs up to your account automatically and syncs across your devices.

    ☁ Cloud sync

    Sync saves your data to a private Gist in your own GitHub account, so your PC and phone always see the same numbers. To connect:

    1. Open github.com/settings/tokens/new (pre-filled with the right settings)
    2. Set expiration to No expiration, scroll down, click Generate token
    3. Copy the token (starts with ghp_) and paste it below

    Paste the same token on your phone (open this site → Sync tab) to link it. The token only has permission to read/write gists — nothing else in your account.

    Connected. Changes save to the cloud automatically a moment after you log them, and the app checks for updates from your other devices every few minutes.

    📄 Import from a spreadsheet

    Coming from Google Sheets or Excel? Export your sheet as a CSV (in Sheets: File → Download → Comma Separated Values), upload it here, confirm the columns, and your history logs itself.

    The tab that's open when you copy the link is the one that imports. To go back to private afterwards, just flip sharing back to Restricted.
